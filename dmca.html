DMCA & Copyright Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AfterDarkXX respects the intellectual property rights of copyright owners and expects users and content providers associated with the website to respect those rights as well.

This policy explains how copyright owners or their authorized representatives can report material that they believe infringes their copyright.

Important:

If you believe that material accessible through AfterDarkXX infringes your copyright, please contact us using the information provided below with enough information for us to identify the material and evaluate your request.

1. Copyright Complaints

We take copyright complaints seriously. Upon receiving a valid copyright infringement notice, we may review the reported material and take appropriate action, which may include disabling access to or removing the reported material where appropriate.

2. What to Include in a Copyright Notice

To help us process your request efficiently, your copyright notice should contain the following information:

  1. A description of the copyrighted work that you believe has been infringed.
  2. The URL or other specific location where the allegedly infringing material can be found.
  3. Your full name and contact information.
  4. A statement confirming that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or applicable law.
  6. A statement that the information provided in your notice is accurate.
  7. Your physical or electronic signature.

3. Review and Removal

After receiving a sufficiently detailed copyright complaint, we will review the information provided and determine what action is appropriate.

Where applicable, reported material may be removed or access to it may be restricted while the complaint is reviewed.

4. Repeat Infringement

We may take appropriate action against accounts, users, or other parties repeatedly associated with valid copyright infringement complaints, where applicable.

5. Third-Party Content and Embeds

Some video content displayed through AfterDarkXX may be provided through third-party services or embedded video players.

AfterDarkXX does not claim ownership of third-party copyrighted material merely because it is accessible through an embedded third-party player.

If your complaint concerns content hosted directly by a third-party service, you may also wish to submit your complaint to the relevant hosting or video service.

6. False or Misleading Notices

Copyright complaints should only be submitted by copyright owners or authorized representatives. Knowingly submitting false or misleading information may have legal consequences.
